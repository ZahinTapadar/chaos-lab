Flow-Lenia creatures that feed, split, convert each other in a cycle and mix their genomes.

Chaos Lab needs WebGPU

Try a recent desktop Chrome or Edge (113+), Safari 26+, or Firefox 141+ on Windows. On Linux, Chrome may need chrome://flags/#enable-unsafe-webgpu. There is no WebGL fallback in this version.
